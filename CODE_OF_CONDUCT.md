# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its community code of conduct.

The full canonical text is maintained at:

- <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
- Plain-text copy: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.txt>

All maintainers, contributors, and participants are expected to read and follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 in their interactions with this project — in the repository, in issues, in pull requests, in documentation, and in any in-person or remote events associated with the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issue tracker, documentation, workshops) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Concerns about behaviour in the project community can be reported confidentially to the Alliance of Bioversity International & CIAT Digital Platforms team. Please include:

- A factual description of what happened (what, where, when)
- Whether the incident is ongoing
- Any supporting evidence you are comfortable sharing (links, screenshots)
- Whether you would like the maintainers to follow up with you

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### How to report

- **Preferred channel:** email the Alliance IBD Digital Platforms team. Until a dedicated contact alias is published, use the contact listed in the project `README.md` or the primary maintainer's GitHub profile.
- **If your concern involves a maintainer:** contact a different maintainer, or contact the Alliance of Bioversity International & CIAT Digital Platforms department directly rather than going through the GitHub issue tracker.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the Contributor Covenant's standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, as described in the Covenant's Enforcement Guidelines.
